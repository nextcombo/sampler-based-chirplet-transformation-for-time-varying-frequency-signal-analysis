# Sampler-based Chirplet Transformation for Time-Varying Frequency Signal Analysis and Its Applications in MATLAB Code

Traditional time-frequency analysis methods, including continuous wavelet transforms, short-time Fourier transforms, and Wigner-Ville distributions, play a significant role in analyzing non-stationary signals. However, due to their poor energy concentration on the time-frequency plane, they often exhibit weak capabilities when dealing with time-varying frequency non-stationary signals. On the other hand, by introducing an additional transformation kernel that matches the instantaneous frequency of the signal, parameterized Time Frequency Analysis (TFA) methods demonstrate strong capabilities in characterizing the time-frequency patterns of non-stationary signals with time-varying frequencies. This paper introduces a novel time-frequency transformation - Sequential Kernel Line Modulated Wavelet Transform (SCT). By incorporating a rotating frequency operator and a shifting frequency operator constructed from spline kernel functions, SCT has strong suppression capabilities for strongly nonlinear modulated signals. Additionally, an effective algorithm is proposed to estimate the transformation kernel parameters within SCT. The effectiveness of SCT and the parameter estimation algorithm is verified through the processing of numerical signals and vibration signals collected from a rotor test bench.
